# Frozen cached-candidate feasibility probe (2026-10-16)

Written before numeric computation. CPU only; no model import, forward, fit, training, GPU or label edits.

Question: does the saved candidate geometry leave room for a pre-final-NMS RoI intervention? Not: can UAV learn the oracle, or will AP improve?

Inputs: frozen 153-image stage summaries and production final boxes for single_best and dual_correct, existing 3734 adjacent pairs and 4496 GT appearances. Stage cache holds only each GT's best eligible box, NOT all eligible candidates. Construct E=deduplicated union of saved best eligible boxes; U=deduplicated E plus final boxes. Coordinate max difference <=1e-4 pixels defines duplicates; stable first occurrence retained. E/U are partial, GT-selected real predicted candidates, not GT boxes. No artificial regression, box union or new proposals.

Fixed IoU thresholds .5 and .75. Matching: per image maximum-cardinality, then maximum summed IoU assignment; pair success requires both GT matched distinctly. Also pair-local feasibility, separated from global assignment. Primary bounded opportunity: a currently globally bbox-failed pair has two distinct U boxes meeting GT IoU threshold AND mutual box IoU<=.5 (fixed deployed NMS threshold). Such two boxes could both survive greedy NMS under an oracle ranking placing them first; this is pair-local only, other instances and false positives unconstrained. Independently report U-feasible but all feasible box pairs mutually suppress, and U-infeasible. These distinguish demonstrated reranking-compatible geometry, fixed-geometry hard-NMS limitation in this subset, and insufficient saved geometry; last category cannot prove new regression/proposals required because cache is incomplete. Report comparison E and final-only and image-wide matching. Never call this complete NMS replay or AP upper bound.

Fixed strata: all; prior interior_nonsmall (both not touching 512 image boundary and mask area>=1024); prior label-risk flagged / unflagged (either member has components_8>1 OR large_secondary_count>0 OR foreign_hole_pixels>0 from existing audit); intersection interior_nonsmall + unflagged. Unflagged is not clean. Each stratum reports appearance counts, unique physical pairs, and physical-pair-equal-weight means of opportunity indicators. Repeated pairs are not independent. No threshold sweep or statistical significance claim.

No learnability pass gate: outcome is capacity evidence only. Nonzero reranking-compatible opportunities support examining local UAV evidence for those cases; they do not authorize training. If zero in interior/unflagged strata, do not promote RoI reranking based on this cache. Save exact source SHA256, script, per-pair records, summary and a report with explicit limitations.
